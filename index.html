FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the first design consultation really free?

Yes. A designer will meet you at your home, office or our studio at no cost and with no obligation.

How long does a typical home interior take?

Most apartment projects are completed in about 45 days after design sign-off, depending on scope.

Do you provide a warranty?

Yes, our modular furniture comes with a warranty of up to 10 years against manufacturing defects.

Can I see the design before work begins?

Absolutely. You'll receive 3D designs and an itemised quote to approve before anything is built.

How much does a 2BHK interior cost in Delhi, Noida, Ghaziabad or Gurgaon?

Most 2BHK homes fall between ₹4.9 lakh and ₹9 lakh depending on the package and scope. Use the cost calculator above for a quick range, then we'll share an itemised quote after a free site visit.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. Your quote lists every item. Civil, electrical and plumbing work is quoted separately before work starts, and any change you ask for later is priced and approved by you first.

Do you charge for the site visit?

No. Site measurement and the first design consultation are free anywhere in Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Gurgaon.

Do you offer EMI?

Yes, EMI options are available on request through partner banks. Ask our team for the current plans.

Will you handle society permissions and work timings?

Yes. We coordinate with your RWA or maintenance office for permissions, follow the society's work hours, book service lifts and clear debris daily.

Do you handle commercial projects?

Yes, we design and build offices, showrooms, restaurants, salons and retail spaces.
